I supervise students whose projects ask how health, poverty and access to services are measured, and what follows from the way the numbers are produced. I work with each student to design a project that is theirs, with a clear question, a feasible data source, and a method they can explain and defend. I expect students to write code that others can rerun, and I encourage them to present early and to publish.

## Prospective students

I welcome enquiries from prospective PhD and master's students interested in spatial statistics, survey methodology, small-area estimation, official statistics, or quantitative global health. Please write to me with a short description of the question you want to study and the data you would use. Doctoral applicants should also say which funding route they plan to pursue, such as a Doctoral Training Partnership studentship, so that we can discuss timing.
