## Main result

The model was applied to health facility surveys in Kenya, Senegal and Tanzania between 1999 and 2020, for readiness and process quality metrics of sick-child care. In out-of-sample cross-validation, the mean absolute error ranged from 3.6% to 4.4% for the readiness metric and from 5.0% to 5.3% for the process quality metric. In Senegal in 2019, the model 95% prediction intervals were narrower than the survey 95% confidence intervals in most departments, especially those with few observations.
