## Main result

The review included 50 studies from 421 papers identified. The primary method was model-based geostatistics in 15 studies, cluster detection or spatial autocorrelation in 14, small-area estimation in 13, and hybrid, machine-learning or other approaches in 8. Most studies relied exclusively on survey data and few discussed how uncertainty in estimates should constrain downstream use. Studies developed in collaboration with national programme teams, which combined routine health system data with household surveys, produced the most operationally relevant outputs. Confirmed programmatic uptake of spatial outputs remained largely undocumented.
