## Main result

Across 72 low- and middle-income countries, coverage of tetanus toxoid-containing vaccination among women with a first live birth averaged 10% before pregnancy, 67% during pregnancy and 76% at birth. Absolute wealth-related inequality, measured by the slope index of inequality, averaged 2.4 percentage points before pregnancy, 10.5 during pregnancy and 12.8 at birth, with higher coverage among wealthier women. During pregnancy it averaged 25.4 percentage points in low-income countries and 10.0 in lower-middle-income countries. In countries that had not achieved maternal and neonatal tetanus elimination, it averaged 45.0 percentage points during pregnancy, against 2.1 in countries that had.
