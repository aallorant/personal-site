## Main result

By the most recent quarter, HIV self-testing accounted for 63% of total testing in Lesotho, 19% to 25% in Malawi and Zimbabwe, and under 15% in Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda and South Africa. In Malawi, Lesotho and Zimbabwe, large volumes of self-testing partly offset declines in conventional testing during the COVID-19 pandemic. In Mozambique, both conventional testing and self-testing expanded. New HIV diagnoses and antiretroviral therapy (ART) initiations remained stable in most settings.
