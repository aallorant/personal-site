- **Lecturer in social statistics and global health**, University of Southampton (2024–), in the Department of Social Statistics and Demography and the Southampton Statistical Sciences Research Institute (S3RI), where I serve as **Deputy Director**
- **Undergraduate Studies Lead**, Department of Social Statistics and Demography (2026–), with responsibility for the undergraduate module offer and programme coordination
- Previously Visiting Assistant Professor in Statistics, Reed College (2023–2024)
- and Postdoctoral Research Fellow, McGill University (2022–2023)
- PhD in Global Health Metrics, University of Washington (2017–2021); Diplôme d'Ingénieur Statisticien-Économètre, ENSAE ParisTech (2013–2016)
- Technical work with the World Health Organization, UNAIDS, Gavi, UNICEF, the World Bank, and several ministries of health
- Current grants include a Scottish Government methodological review of sampling and weighting for its flagship population surveys (co-PI, £40k, 2026–27) and a University of Southampton Global Partnership Award for immunisation-coverage work with partners in Mozambique and the Democratic Republic of the Congo (PI, £9,016, 2026–27)
- Member of the UNAIDS Reference Group on Estimates, Modelling and Projections
- Fellow of the Royal Statistical Society; board member of its International Development Section
- Fellow of Advance HE (FHEA)
